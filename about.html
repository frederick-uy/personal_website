About

Hi, I'm Frederick.

I bought my first DSLR in high school and photographed friends and family. Over 7 years that turned into FMU Photography, with portraits, graduations, proposals and 10+ weddings.

My career took a different route: an HBCUvc fellowship, seven internships across audit, treasury and analytics, then Google's finance rotation program. That led to data engineering, most recently as a Data Engineer III.

Both jobs are about noticing what matters and presenting it clearly. I'm now back home in Tacoma and looking for my next data role in the Seattle area.
