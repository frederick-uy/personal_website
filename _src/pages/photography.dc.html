Good to know

First time in front of a camera?+

You’re in good company. I’ll guide you through every pose.

Where do you shoot?+

Around Tacoma and Seattle. Travel outside the area on request.

What if it rains?+

Light rain can be beautiful, so if you’re up for it, I’ll bring umbrellas and we’ll lean into the mood. If it’s pouring or you’d rather stay dry, we’ll reschedule for free to the next day that works for both of us.

Can I bring a partner or friends?+

Yes. Add a partner for $50, or book a family session for 3–5 people.
