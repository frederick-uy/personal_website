Problem → approach → result

Google · Core Strategy & Operations

One headcount number everyone trusts

Problem: HR, recruiting and finance data lived in systems that didn’t integrate, so there was no single view of headcount.

Approach: I integrated the three sources and aligned definitions with program managers across the full headcount lifecycle: open roles, hires without requisitions and campus hires.

Result

One source of truth used by executives, finance and recruiting partners, chiefs of staff and cost center owners

Google · Core Strategy & Operations

Email as a reporting service

Problem: Leaders needed regular headcount and recruiting updates, and my first version in Apps Script hit its limits with internal data.

Approach: I evaluated internal tools and rebuilt the reports as scheduled workflows that render HTML/CSS emails from live data.

Why email? A metric only matters if someone acts on it, so my reports go to inboxes, not just dashboards.

Result

30 automated reports, including a weekly director newsletter on headcount and recruiting

Google · Core Strategy & Operations

An AI agent for org-health questions

Problem: Leaders wanted to ask the headcount data questions directly, in plain language.

Approach: I built the team’s first chatbot (Python, NotebookLM) and grew it into a RAG-based agent that answers quantitative and qualitative questions, like “What’s the ratio of program managers to engineers?”

Result

Used by the chief of staff team and all 11 pillars
